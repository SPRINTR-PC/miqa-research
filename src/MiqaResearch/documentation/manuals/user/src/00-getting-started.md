# Getting started

<!-- PROTOTYPE stub page for the manual layout prototype (#233). Not real content. -->

MIQA Research collects consent, clinical forms and imaging for research projects. This
page walks you through signing in and finding your projects.

## Signing in

Use **Sign in** in the top bar. Depending on your installation you sign in with your
organisation account, BankID or a local password.

:::{note}
If you cannot see a project you expect, ask the project's Admin to add you.
:::

## Finding your projects

Open **Dashboard**. Every project you are a member of is listed, with its sites and goals.

| Role | Can see | Can change |
|------|---------|------------|
| Researcher | Own sites | Forms |
| Data Manager | All sites | Data, pipelines |
| Admin | Everything | Everything |

## Switching theme

The moon/sun button toggles dark and light mode.
